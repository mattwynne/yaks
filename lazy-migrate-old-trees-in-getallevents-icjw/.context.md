In git.rs get_all_events(), when walking history backwards and
reading a commit's tree, check .schema-version. If it's older
than CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION, apply the migration chain to that
tree before reading snapshots/fields from it.

This is the core change — it lets the reader handle old-format
trees without needing a Migrated boundary to hide them.

Key files: git.rs:386 (get_all_events), migration.rs (migration
chain), tree.rs (read_snapshots_from_tree).
